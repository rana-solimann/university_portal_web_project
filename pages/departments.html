Departments

Dream University offers two academic tracks to choose your path!


Credit Hour Departments

Information Security

Focuses on protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats. Students learn ethical hacking, cryptography, and risk management.

Network

Covers the design, implementation, and management of computer networks. Students gain hands-on experience with routing, switching, and network security.



Mainstream Departments

Computer Science

A comprehensive program covering algorithms, data structures, software engineering, and programming. Prepares students for a wide range of tech careers.

Artificial Intelligence

Explores machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and computer vision. Prepares students to build the intelligent systems of tomorrow.
