About Dream University


Dream University was founded in 1952 in the heart of the Arab Republic of Dreams, with a vision to become a leading academic institution dedicated to excellence in education, research, and community service.


Our Vision

To be a globally recognized university that inspires innovation, nurtures talent, and builds future leaders who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

Dream University is committed to providing high-quality education through modern curricula, qualified faculty, and a stimulating academic environment that encourages creativity and critical thinking.

President's Word

"At Dream University, we believe that education is the foundation of every dream. Our goal is to empower every student with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to turn their dreams into reality." Prof. Ahmed Al-Rashidi, University President

History

Established over 70 years ago, Dream University began as a small college with 3 faculties and 500 students. Today it has grown into a comprehensive university with 18 faculties, 40,000 students, and partnerships with over 50 international universities.

Facts & Figures

  • 18 Faculties
  • 40,000+ Students
  • 2,500 Academic Staff
  • 50+ International Partnerships
  • 70+ Years of Excellence
